A short one again. Event Book got a few small changes to how a board looks, and Count me in moved to a new home.

## Event Book: a bit of tidying

Board columns show their slice number now (S1, S2 and so on). There was a follow-up fix so the number is drawn from the slice's data and no longer depends on its name text.

The Neat style also uses one sans font now. And old agents that showed up as "Claude on" plus a hostname show as ItsyBOT instead.

## Count me in

Count me in moved to its own address, and the old prototype address forwards old links there. The studio got a card for it, with its build book on the shelf next to the other apps.

## barrio

A reply picture is now a tiny collage of all the photos in it, and the banderitas are gone from it.

## Reflection

**What went well:** Several small things went live in a day, in four repos.

**What could be better:** This post was written from PR titles again, so there's not much about why things changed.

**Shipped:**
- Event Book: slice numbers on board columns, Neat style with one sans font, old agents shown as ItsyBOT
- Count me in: new address with forwarding from the old one, card and build book in the studio
- barrio: reply picture as a collage of photos
